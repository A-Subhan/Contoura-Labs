1. Introduction

Contoura Labs operates a collection of free, browser-based tools, games, and informational content available at contouralabs.com. Our guiding privacy principle is simple: your data should never have to leave your device to use our tools.

All file-processing tools (PDF converters, image compressors, QR generators, etc.) execute entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Files you select are read into your browser's memory locally and processed on your own device. They are not transmitted to our servers or any third-party server.

This approach means that, for the vast majority of our tools, we do not collect, store, or have access to the content you process. The limited information we do collect — described in the sections below — is used solely to operate and improve the website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect a minimal set of information needed to operate the website and respond to user inquiries. We categorize the information we may collect as follows:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

If you use the contact form on our FAQ & Contact page, we collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, subject, and message. This information is sent through the EmailJS service to our team email and used solely to respond to your inquiry.

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect personal information simply to use the tools or games.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, our hosting provider and analytics services may automatically record certain technical information, including:

  • Your IP address (typically truncated or anonymized by our analytics provider)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • The pages you visit and the duration of your visit
  • The referring website (the page you came from)
  • Approximate geographic region (country-level, derived from IP)

This information is used in aggregate to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve performance, content, and usability. It is not used to identify individual users.

2.3 Information from Files You Process

We do not collect, transmit, or store the contents of files you process with our tools. All file operations — including PDF merging, image compression, format conversion, and similar — happen entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

The only exceptions are tools that require external data to function (such as the currency converter, which fetches live exchange rates, or the speed test, which measures connection quality). In those cases, only the specific data needed for that operation is transmitted, and no file content is ever sent.

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries: When you contact us, we use your name, email, and message to reply.
  • To operate and maintain the website: Technical data helps us monitor uptime, diagnose bugs, and ensure compatibility across browsers.
  • To improve our tools and content: Aggregate analytics help us understand which tools are most used, where users encounter errors, and what new tools to build next.
  • To ensure security and prevent abuse: We may use technical data to detect and block automated abuse, scrapers, or malicious traffic.
  • To comply with legal obligations: If required by law, we may retain or disclose information to authorities.

We do not use your information to build behavioral profiles, sell to third parties, or for targeted advertising beyond the contextual ads served by our advertising partners (see the Third-Party Services section).

We do not use your data to train machine learning models of any kind.

4. Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies and similar browser-storage technologies to operate the website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.

The cookies we use fall into three categories:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (e.g., remembering your dark-mode preference).
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how the site is used (e.g., anonymous visit counts).
  • Advertising cookies: Used by our advertising partners (e.g., Google AdSense) to display relevant ads.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the website. For more detail, please review our Cookie Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted third-party services to operate the website. Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data. We encourage you to review their policies.

5.1 Web Hosting & CDN

The website is served through a content delivery network (CDN) that may log basic request information (IP address, request time, user agent) for security and performance purposes.

5.2 Analytics

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. Our analytics provider processes anonymized or pseudonymized data and does not track individual users across other websites.

5.3 Advertising (Google AdSense)

We participate in Google AdSense to display ads that help fund our free tools. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by using the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out.

5.4 EmailJS

Our contact form uses EmailJS to deliver messages to our team inbox. When you submit the form, your name, email, subject, and message are transmitted to EmailJS and forwarded to our email provider. EmailJS does not use this information for marketing.

5.5 External CDN Libraries

Many of our tools load JavaScript libraries (pdf-lib, JSZip, QRCode.js, etc.) from public CDNs such as jsDelivr and unpkg. These CDNs may log request metadata (IP, time, user agent) for their own operational purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited information we collect. These measures include:

  • Serving the website over HTTPS with TLS encryption.
  • Restricting access to backend systems and analytics on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regularly updating dependencies and reviewing security advisories.
  • Using reputable third-party services with established security practices.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

The most significant security feature of our architecture is that your file content is never transmitted to us in the first place. All tool processing happens in your browser, which means there is no server-side database of user files for an attacker to breach.

For more detail on our security approach, please see our Security page.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding the personal information we hold about you. These may include:

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure: You may request that we delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to object: You may object to our processing of your information for certain purposes.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Right to data portability: You may request a machine-readable copy of your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contouralabs@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The tools and games on our site are appropriate for general audiences and do not request age or other identifying information from minors.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at contouralabs@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete such information.

For users in the United States, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). For users in the EU and UK, we comply with the GDPR's provisions regarding minors.

If we make significant changes that affect your rights, we will provide notice through the website or via email (if you have contacted us previously).
