The short version: Almost every tool on Contoura Labs runs entirely inside your browser. We never receive your files, your text inputs, or the contents of anything you upload. The only information we collect is anonymous usage data and the information you choose to give us when you contact us.

01 Introduction

Contoura Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at contouralabs.com along with all subdomains, web tools, browser based games, and related services (collectively, the "Service"). The Service is provided free of charge and is designed to process your data locally inside your web browser whenever possible.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we do and do not collect when you use the Service, how any collected information is used, and the rights you have over that information. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the Service. This policy is intended to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other applicable privacy laws, and in many cases goes beyond what those laws require.

02 Information We Collect

We collect an extremely limited amount of information, and we never collect the contents of files that you process with our tools. The categories of information we may collect are described below.

  • Information you provide directly: When you contact us through our contact form, we collect the name, email address, subject, and message body that you submit. We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • Anonymous usage data: We collect aggregated, anonymous statistics about which pages are visited, which tools are used, and general error rates. This data cannot be tied back to an individual user.
  • Technical data: Our hosting provider may log standard request information such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp for security and abuse prevention purposes. These logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days.
  • Theme preference: Your light or dark mode preference is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • What we do not collect: We do not collect or transmit the contents of files you upload to any tool, the text you type into text tools, the images you edit, or the documents you convert. All processing happens in your browser.

03 How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect for the following specific purposes.

  • To respond to enquiries and provide support when you contact us through the contact form or by email.
  • To monitor and improve the performance, stability, and usability of the Service through anonymous aggregated analytics.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to security issues, fraud, or abuse of the Service.
  • To maintain and operate the Service, including serving pages, processing contact form submissions, and keeping the site online.
  • To comply with our legal obligations and to enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, we do not sell or rent your information to anyone, and we do not use your information to train machine learning models.

04 Cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. We use a minimal number of cookies, and we never use cookies to track you across other websites.

  • Essential cookies: These are required for the website to function, such as remembering your theme preference. They cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: If you consent, we may use anonymous analytics cookies to understand which tools are most used and where visitors encounter errors.
  • Advertising cookies: If we display ads on a page, our advertising partner may set cookies. These are governed by the partner own privacy policy and are not controlled by us.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. A full list of every cookie we set, along with its purpose and lifetime, is available in our Cookie Policy.

05 Third Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted third party services to keep the Service online and to deliver core functionality. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

  • Hosting provider: The server that delivers our HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files to your browser. The provider may log request metadata for security purposes.
  • Email delivery: We use a third party service to deliver messages sent through our contact form to our inbox. The contents of your message pass through this service on the way to us.
  • Analytics provider: We use a privacy friendly analytics service that does not use cookies to identify individuals and does not sell data to third parties.
  • Advertising partner: Where ads appear, they are delivered by a third party advertising network. The network may set its own cookies and process data according to its own policy.
  • Content delivery network: Static assets such as fonts and JavaScript libraries may be served from a CDN. The CDN operator may log request metadata.

We do not share your personal information with any third party for their own marketing purposes, and we have signed data processing agreements with each of the providers above where required by law.

06 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect any information we hold. These measures include encryption of data in transit using HTTPS, encryption of any stored data at rest, strict access controls that limit who at Contoura Labs can access contact form submissions, and regular security reviews of our codebase.

The single most important security feature of Contoura Labs, however, is that we do not collect your tool data in the first place. Because every tool runs in your browser, the files you upload, the text you convert, and the images you edit never travel across the internet to us. We cannot lose, leak, or be compelled to hand over data we never had.

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting the limited information we do hold, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities without undue delay, in accordance with applicable law. More details about our security practices are available on our Security page.

07 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights over the personal information we hold about you. These rights typically include the following.

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of any personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Right to erasure: You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to limited legal exceptions.
  • Right to object: You can object to our processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: You can request your personal information in a structured, machine readable format.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where we rely on your consent for processing, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in section 10 below. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

08 Children Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our games are family friendly and safe for children to use, but children should use the Service under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will promptly delete that information. We will also take reasonable steps to prevent the collection of such information in the future.

For users in the European Union, the age of digital consent varies by member state. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, you must obtain parental consent before using the contact form or otherwise providing us with personal information.
