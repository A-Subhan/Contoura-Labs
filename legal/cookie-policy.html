02 What Are Cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. Each cookie contains a name, a value, an expiry date, and the domain it belongs to. Cookies allow a website to remember your actions and preferences over time, which makes the website more useful and pleasant to use.

Cookies are not programs and cannot run code on your computer. They cannot read files from your hard drive, they cannot access your personal information beyond what you have explicitly provided to the website, and they cannot be used to install software. They are simply small pieces of text.

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as local storage and session storage. These work in much the same way but allow us to store slightly more information in your browser. References to "cookies" in this policy include these technologies unless otherwise stated.

03 How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for a small number of specific, clearly defined purposes. We never use cookies to identify you personally, to build advertising profiles, or to track your activity across other websites.

  • To keep the website working: Some cookies are required for basic functionality, such as remembering your light or dark theme preference between visits.
  • To understand how the website is used: We use anonymous analytics to see which tools are most popular and where visitors run into errors, so we can improve the Service.
  • To display advertising: On a small number of pages, our advertising partner may set cookies to display relevant ads. These are governed by the partner own privacy policy.
  • To prevent abuse: We may use cookies to detect and prevent automated abuse, fraud, or excessive requests to our servers.

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information such as health data, political opinions, religious beliefs, or sexual orientation, and we do not combine cookie data with such information from any other source.

04 Types of Cookies We Use

The cookies we use fall into four broad categories. Each cookie on our site belongs to one of these categories.

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function. Without them, features like theme preference and form state would not work. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference cookies: Remember choices you make, such as your preferred theme or recently used tool settings. These are optional but improve your experience.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the website so we can improve it. The data collected is aggregated and anonymous.
  • Advertising cookies: Used by our advertising partner, where applicable, to display ads that are more relevant to your interests. These are governed by the partner policy.

A full list of every cookie we set, including its name, purpose, category, and lifetime, is provided in the table near the bottom of this policy.

05 Third Party Cookies

In addition to the cookies we set ourselves, a small number of trusted third party services may set cookies when you visit our website. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they use the data they collect.

  • Analytics provider: We use a privacy friendly analytics service that collects aggregated, anonymous usage data. It does not use cookies to identify individuals.
  • Advertising network: Where ads are shown, the advertising network may set its own cookies. You can usually opt out of personalised advertising through the network own settings page.
  • Content delivery network: Our CDN provider may set cookies to optimise the delivery of static assets such as fonts and JavaScript files.
  • Email service: If you submit our contact form, the email delivery service we use may process your submission according to its own policy.

We do not control the cookies set by third parties and have no access to the data they collect. We have, however, made every reasonable effort to select partners whose privacy practices align with our own.

06 Managing and Deleting Cookies

You have full control over cookies stored in your browser. You can delete them at any time, and you can configure your browser to refuse them entirely. The exact process depends on which browser you use.

  • Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences, Privacy, Cookies and website data.
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions, Cookies and site data.

Please note that disabling all cookies may affect the functionality of the website. For example, your theme preference will not be remembered, and certain tools that rely on local storage may behave differently. Most browsers allow you to block third party cookies while still allowing first party cookies, which is a good compromise.

If you want to opt out of personalised advertising from third party advertising networks, you can usually do so through the network own opt out page or through the Digital Advertising Alliance consumer choice page.
