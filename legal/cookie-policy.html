1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device when you visit them. They allow the website to recognize your browser across sessions, remember your preferences, and gather anonymous usage statistics. Cookies are widely used across the web and are not inherently harmful — they are simply a mechanism for storing small amounts of data on your device.

Cookies typically contain a name, a value, an expiration date, and the domain they belong to. They can be either "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain on your device until they expire or you delete them).

In addition to cookies, websites may use other browser-storage technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage. These work similarly to cookies but can store larger amounts of data. Contoura Labs uses localStorage to remember your dark-mode preference and any tool-specific settings (e.g., previously entered values in calculators).

For simplicity, in this policy we use the term "cookies" to refer to all of these technologies collectively.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for a small number of specific purposes:

  • Essential functionality: Remembering your theme (dark/light) preference, preserving tool settings during your session, and keeping the contact form secure.
  • Analytics: Counting visits, understanding which tools are most popular, and diagnosing errors — all in aggregate, anonymously.
  • Advertising: Allowing our advertising partner (Google AdSense) to display and measure ads, and to prevent the same ad from being shown to you too frequently.

We do not use cookies to track your activity across other websites for the purpose of building a behavioral profile, beyond what is required by our advertising partner to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time (see the Managing Cookies section below).

We do not use cookies to store any information about the files you process with our tools. All file processing happens in your browser's memory and is discarded when you close the tab.

4. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we allow certain third-party services to set cookies through our website. These third parties use cookies to provide their services to us and to you.

4.1 Google AdSense

Google AdSense is our advertising partner. When you visit a page that displays ads, Google may set or read cookies in your browser to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4.2 Analytics Provider

Our analytics provider may set a cookie to distinguish unique visitors. This cookie does not contain personally identifiable information and is used solely to compute aggregate metrics.

4.3 CDN-Hosted Libraries

We load JavaScript libraries (such as pdf-lib, JSZip, and Tailwind CSS) from public CDNs like jsDelivr and unpkg. These CDNs may set their own cookies to track usage patterns and improve performance. We do not control these cookies; please review the CDN's privacy policy for more information.

4.4 EmailJS

When you submit our contact form, your message is transmitted through EmailJS. EmailJS does not typically set cookies, but their service may log request metadata for operational purposes.

5. Managing Cookies

You have several options for managing or deleting cookies. Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • View all cookies stored on your device.
  • Delete individual or all cookies from a particular website.
  • Block cookies from specific websites or all websites.
  • Set preferences for first-party vs. third-party cookies.
  • Enable "Do Not Track" signals (though not all websites respond to these).

5.2 Opting Out of Personalized Ads

If you'd prefer not to see personalized ads, you can opt out using the following industry opt-out tools:

  • Google Ads Settings — opt out of Google's personalized ads.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out — opt out of participating ad networks.
  • Your Online Choices (EU) — opt out for European users.

5.3 Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable all cookies, the basic functionality of our tools will continue to work (since they don't rely on cookies for processing), but you may experience the following limitations:

  • Your theme preference will not be remembered between visits.
  • Tool-specific settings (e.g., last-used page size) will not persist.
  • The contact form may require additional verification steps to prevent spam.
  • You may see less relevant advertising.

Essential cookies cannot be disabled; if you do not want these set, you should not use the website.
