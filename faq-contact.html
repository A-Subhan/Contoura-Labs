General
Are the tools really free?

Yes — every tool on Contoura Labs is 100% free to use, with no hidden fees, no trial periods, and no credit card required. We don't have a "premium" tier or upsell. The tools are funded by occasional, non-intrusive ads.

There are also no usage limits. Convert as many PDFs, generate as many QR codes, or play as many games as you like.

Account
Do I need to create an account?

No account is required for any of our tools or games. Just visit the page and start using it. We don't ask for your name, email, or any personal information to use the tools.

If you contact us through the form below, we'll only use your email to reply to your message.

Privacy
Is my data safe when using the tools?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Files you upload (PDFs, images, documents) never leave your device — they are not uploaded to any server.

This means your data is as safe as your own device. You can even use the tools offline once the page has loaded. Read more in our Security page and Privacy Policy.

Usage
Can I use these tools offline?

Most tools work offline once the page is loaded, because all processing happens in your browser. However, you'll need an internet connection for the initial page load (to fetch Tailwind CSS, fonts, and any tool-specific libraries from a CDN).

A few tools that rely on external APIs — like the currency converter or speed test — require a live internet connection to function.

Feedback
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We love hearing from users. Use the contact form below to send us bug reports, feature requests, or general feedback. Please include as much detail as possible:

• Which tool you were using
• What you expected to happen
• What actually happened
• Your browser and operating system

We read every message and prioritize fixes based on impact.

Services
Do you offer custom web development services?

Yes! Contoura Labs is also a small web development studio. We take on selective client work including landing pages, web apps, e-commerce sites, and custom browser-based tools.

Send us a brief via the contact form below with your project scope, timeline, and budget range. We'll get back to you within 24 hours to discuss whether we're a good fit.

Technical
What technologies do you use?

Our tools are built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3. We use Tailwind CSS for styling and a handful of CDN-hosted libraries for specific tasks (pdf-lib, JSZip, QRCode.js, mammoth, browser-image-compression, etc.).

For client projects we typically use Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Prisma, depending on requirements.

Services
How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks. A typical marketing site or small web app takes 3–6 weeks. Larger applications with backend, authentication, and integrations can run 2–4 months.

We'll give you a realistic timeline after the discovery call — and we stick to it.

Services
Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes. We offer monthly maintenance retainers for sites and apps we've built. This covers security updates, dependency upgrades, small content changes, performance monitoring, and priority bug fixes.

If you have a site built by someone else, we may still be able to help — reach out and we'll take a look.

Services
What is your refund policy?

Since our tools are free, there's nothing to refund. For custom client work: if we haven't started yet, you can cancel for a full refund. Once work has begun, we bill for hours completed up to the cancellation date and refund the rest.

For monthly retainers, you can cancel anytime before the next billing cycle.
