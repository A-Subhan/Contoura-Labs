Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how Contoura Labs works, what we do with your data, and how to get the most out of our tools. Still stuck? Send us a note using the contact form below.

01 Are your tools really free to use?

Yes. Every tool, game, and resource on Contoura Labs is completely free. There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no daily usage limits. We believe useful software should be accessible to everyone.

02 Do I need to create an account to use the tools?

No account is required. You can open any tool or game and start using it immediately. We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal information before, during, or after you use a tool.

03 Where are my files stored when I use a tool?

Nowhere except on your own device. Every tool processes your files entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. Your documents, images, and text never leave your computer and are never uploaded to any server we control.

04 Can I use these tools on my phone or tablet?

Absolutely. The entire site is fully responsive and touch friendly. Every tool and game has been designed to work on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops with no loss of functionality. Layouts reflow naturally to fit any screen size.

05 Do you show ads on the tools and games?

We may show lightweight, non intrusive ads on a small number of pages to cover server and domain costs. Ads are never placed inside the tool work area and they never interfere with the functionality of any tool. You will never see a pop up, an interstitial, or a video that blocks the page.

06 Why does the background remover take a long time to load the first time?

The background remover downloads a machine learning model that is roughly 80 megabytes in size on first use. This model runs entirely in your browser. After the first load, your browser caches it, so subsequent uses are much faster and require no additional download.

07 Can I use the tools offline?

Most tools can be used offline after the page has loaded at least once, because the JavaScript runs locally in your browser. However, the page itself must be loaded from our server the first time you visit, and a small number of tools that fetch live data, such as the currency converter, will always need an active internet connection.

08 Do you offer an API for your tools?

We do not currently offer a public API. All processing happens in the browser, which means there is no server endpoint to call. If you need to integrate similar logic into your own application, the underlying libraries we use are open source and are listed at the bottom of each tool page.

09 How often do you add new tools?

We aim to ship at least one new tool or game every week. You can follow the blog or check the tools and games hub pages to see what has been added recently. We also announce new releases on our social channels.

10 Can I suggest a new tool idea?

Yes, please do. Use the contact form on this page and tell us what you are trying to accomplish. We read every single message and prioritise ideas that would be useful to many people. If your suggestion ships, we will credit you in the launch blog post if you would like.

11 Are the games safe for children?

Yes. All of our games are family friendly, contain no graphic violence beyond classic arcade action, and do not include any in app purchases, chat features, or external links. They are safe for players of all ages and can be handed to a child without supervision.

12 What browsers do you support?

We support the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Some advanced tools that use modern web APIs may not work in older browsers, but everything is tested across the major modern browsers before release. If you spot a bug on a recent browser, please let us know through the form below.
